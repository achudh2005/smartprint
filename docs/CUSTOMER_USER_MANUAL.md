# SmartPrint Customer User Manual

Welcome to **SmartPrint**! SmartPrint provides a contactless, secure, zero-friction printing experience. You do **not** need to create an account, log in, share your phone number, or provide an email address.

---

## 1. How It Works (Quick 4-Step Process)

```
[Scan Shop QR] ──> [Upload Documents] ──> [Choose Print Options & Expiry] ──> [Pay & Track Live]
```

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Scan the Shop's QR Code
1. Point your smartphone camera at the physical QR code displayed at the printing shop.
2. Tap the link to open the shop's customer portal (e.g., `https://smartprint.example.com/s/CPHUB1`).
3. You will immediately see the shop name, logo, supported file formats, and upload guidelines.

---

### Step 2: Upload Your Documents
1. Tap or click **"Choose Files"** or drag and drop your documents into the upload box.
2. **Supported Formats:**
   - PDF Documents (`.pdf`)
   - Word Documents (`.docx`, `.doc`)
   - Images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`)
3. The system will inspect your file, automatically detect the total page count, and display an immediate visual preview.

> [!NOTE]
> Corrupted or password-protected files are detected instantly with clear guidance to unprotect the file before re-uploading.

---

### Step 3: Configure Print Settings & Services
For each uploaded document, configure:
- **Copies**: Select number of copies (e.g., 1, 2, 5).
- **Color Mode**: Choose **Black & White** or **Full Color**.
- **Paper Size**: A4, A3, Letter, or Legal.
- **Two-Sided (Duplex)**: Single-Sided or Double-Sided (back-to-back).
- **Page Range**:
  - `ALL`: Prints all pages.
  - Custom ranges: e.g., `1-5, 8, 11-14`. The page counter automatically recalculates the exact number of pages to be printed.
- **Additional Finishing Services**:
  - Soft Spiral Binding
  - Hardcover Project Binding
  - Document Lamination
  - Corner Stapling

The price breakdown updates dynamically in real time according to the shop's active pricing rules.

---

### Step 4: Choose Document Privacy Lifetime
Protect your personal files by selecting how long the shop may retain your document:
- **15 Minutes** (Recommended for sensitive documents like Aadhaar, Passports, Bank Statements).
- **1 Hour** (Standard default).
- **3 Hours**
- **8 Hours**

Once this time expires, your document is **permanently purged** from the server and cannot be accessed or downloaded by anyone.

---

### Step 5: Submit Order & Choose Payment Method
1. Review your order total and itemized breakdown.
2. Choose your payment method:
   - **Pay Online (UPI / Card / NetBanking / Wallet)**: Powered securely by Razorpay. Your order will be confirmed immediately upon successful payment.
   - **Pay at Counter (Cash / Counter UPI)**: Submit your order and pay the shopkeeper directly at the billing counter.
3. You will receive a unique **Order Number** (e.g., `ORD-2026-1042`).

---

### Step 6: Real-Time Order Tracking
Keep the confirmation page open to monitor your order progress in real time:
- 🟡 **Pending**: Waiting for shop confirmation.
- 🔵 **Accepted**: Shop operator reviewed and accepted your files.
- 🖨️ **Printing**: Document is currently printing on the physical machine.
- 🟢 **Ready for Pickup**: Your physical prints are ready at the counter! Collect your copies and show your Order Number.
- ✅ **Completed**: Order picked up and finalized.

---

## 3. Privacy Guarantee
SmartPrint operates on a **Zero-Trust Privacy Architecture**:
- Your documents are never indexed or shared.
- Upon expiry, files are immediately unlinked from storage.
- If you cancel an order before printing starts, your files are wiped immediately.
